# The use case routes work without absorbing it

The learner can trace Ready to evaluation, NeedsReview to a review queue, and Rejected to diagnostics. They understand that the use case coordinates these collaborators while the evaluation policy owns scoring and the presenter owns output formatting.

## Evidence

The learner reported that they can trace all three outcomes, identify the next collaborator, and keep scoring rules and output formatting out of the use case.

## Implications

The course can now implement an executable vertical slice and use compiler and test feedback to verify these responsibility boundaries.
