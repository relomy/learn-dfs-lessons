# Candidate assembly has explicit outcomes

The learner understands that candidate assembly determines whether normalized, identity-resolved records can safely form a slate candidate. They distinguish pass, hold, and drop, and understand that this is a responsibility that may begin as a use-case function rather than a dedicated class.

## Evidence

The learner correctly classified five novel cases: partial statistics and optional enrichment pass; ambiguous identity holds; invalid or absent required salary drops. They also explained that the evaluation policy, not assembly, decides whether unknown passing attempts prevent scoring.

## Implications

The next lesson can model the three assembly outcomes as explicit application results rather than a boolean, null, or silent omission.
