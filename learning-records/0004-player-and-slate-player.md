# Player identity is distinct from slate participation

The learner understands that `Player` represents a real person with an application-owned stable identity, while `SlatePlayer` represents that player's participation in one slate. They chose `SlatePlayer` as a descriptive domain name for the slate-specific concept containing facts such as matchup and salary.

## Evidence

The learner identified the naming issue rather than the boundary as the source of uncertainty, then accepted the distinction and selected a name based on the first-slice meaning.
