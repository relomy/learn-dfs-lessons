# Evaluation policy owns domain scoring rules

The learner understands that player selection and player evaluation are distinct responsibilities, that scoring rules belong in a domain-owned evaluation policy, and that a simple explicit policy should precede a strategy abstraction until meaningful variation appears.

## Evidence

The learner correctly answered the quarterback evaluation quiz and transferred the same reasoning to a meal-delivery ranking scenario, identifying source translation, use-case selection, evaluation policy, and the appropriate point for introducing a strategy abstraction.
