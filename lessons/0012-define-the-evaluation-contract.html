Define the evaluation contract

Make the scoring policy’s inputs and outputs explicit.

The idea

Before adding formulas, define what an evaluation policy needs and what it promises to return. This contract prevents a scoring rule from quietly reaching into CSV rows, making undocumented assumptions, or confusing missing data with a zero value.

Contract question:
What must be known to evaluate this player, and what does the caller receive when that information is unavailable?

Separate the data roles

  • Raw source data: provider-shaped values such as CSV strings and column names.
  • Domain facts: translated values such as salary, matchup, and availability.
  • Evaluation inputs: the selected facts and statistics the policy actually needs.
  • Evaluation result: a domain-owned score, rating, or explicit “not evaluable” outcome.

Missing data is a modeling decision. Treating an unknown statistic as zero may produce a valid number with an invalid meaning. The contract should make that choice visible.
