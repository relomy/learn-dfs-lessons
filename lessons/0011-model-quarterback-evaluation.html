Lesson 11 · DFS analytics architecture

Model quarterback evaluation

Give the first domain behavior a clear home.

The idea

Loading players and evaluating players are different application concerns. A use case coordinates the work; a domain policy explains how an evaluation is calculated. Keeping that distinction clear lets scoring rules evolve without making the source adapter responsible for them.

Start small:
One explicit quarterback scoring policy is more valuable than an abstraction for every future position.

Separate the responsibilities

  • Source adapter: obtains and translates slate facts.
  • Use case: chooses the players to evaluate, supplies the required inputs, and presents the result.
  • Evaluation policy: applies the rules that turn domain facts into an evaluation.

For now, “evaluation” can remain deliberately simple—for example, a transparent formula using available statistics. The important design decision is who owns the meaning of that formula.
