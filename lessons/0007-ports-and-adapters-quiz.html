Ports and adapters quiz

Retrieve the boundary ideas before adding another source.

Quick context

An adapter translates between the application and an external system. A port is the application-owned boundary that describes what the application needs, without naming DraftKings or another provider. Together, they make the source replaceable at the edge.

Dependency direction:
The application defines the need; an external adapter satisfies it.
