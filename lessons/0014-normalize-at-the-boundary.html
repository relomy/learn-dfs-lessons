Lesson 14 · DFS analytics architecture

Normalize at the boundary

Translate provider-shaped records into stable, domain-owned evaluation inputs.

The idea

Identity resolution tells us which records refer to the same player. Normalization tells us what those records mean in our application. Both should happen before data enters the evaluation core.

Boundary rule:
External formats may vary; domain meaning should remain stable.

Example

DraftKings: {"passYds": "280", "salary": "$7,400"}
        ↓ adapter translation
EvaluationInput: {passing_yards: 280, salary: 7400}

The evaluator should not know that one source calls the field passYds or encodes salary with a dollar sign.

Make uncertainty visible

A missing statistic is not automatically zero. Zero means the measured value is zero; missing means the value is unavailable. The normalized input should preserve that distinction so the evaluation policy can decide whether the player is evaluable.
