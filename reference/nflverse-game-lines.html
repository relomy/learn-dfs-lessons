nflverse game lines

Provider vocabulary stays at the boundary.

Fields we need

away_team
home_team
spread_line
total_line

Sign conversion

nflverse uses a positive spread_line when the home team is favored. Our domain uses a negative homeSpread when the home team is favored.

VegasGameLine.homeSpread = -NflverseGameRow.spread_line
VegasGameLine.gameTotal = NflverseGameRow.total_line

Missing is not zero

A missing spread_line or total_line means the source did not supply a usable line. A value of zero is a real pick'em spread. Reject or explicitly report missing data at the adapter boundary.

Scope

The public CSV is a good historical and backtesting source. Do not treat scheduled rows with blank line fields as live odds.
