Assembly outcomes

Represent the next action explicitly when candidate assembly succeeds, needs review, or rejects input.

Result rule:
A result must tell the next component what to do and include the information needed to do it.

Ready

Contains the domain candidate. It may retain unknown values that the evaluation policy owns.

Needs review

Contains a machine-readable reason, the affected source references, and enough context for a person or later process to resolve the identity question.

Rejected

Contains a reason and source context for diagnostics. It does not contain a candidate that another component might accidentally evaluate.

Avoid ambiguous signals

A boolean cannot say why assembly failed. A null cannot distinguish reviewable uncertainty from a permanent rejection. An explicit result makes those paths visible to callers and tests.
