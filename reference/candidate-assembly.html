Candidate assembly

Join normalized records into a domain candidate while keeping uncertainty and rejection visible.

Assembly rule:
The assembler decides whether records can form a candidate. The evaluation policy decides how an eligible candidate scores.

Inputs

  • records linked to the same player and slate;
  • domain-owned values from source adapters; and
  • the known state of each value, including unknown or invalid.

Outcomes

  • Pass: create a candidate and preserve any uncertainty that the evaluation policy owns.
  • Hold: stop for identity or other review that a person or later process may resolve.
  • Drop: do not create a candidate because a required input is invalid or absent.

Do not collapse these

An unresolved identity is not an invalid value. An unknown passing-attempt count is not zero. A candidate that reaches the evaluation policy is not necessarily scored. Each has a different owner and a different next action.
